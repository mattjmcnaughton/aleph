The last Mark complete should not look like the first

Five directions for the moment a learner taps Mark complete on the final lesson of a path, drawn in Nocturne against the surface that renders today. Every phone below is live — press Replay to run the motion.

Vocabulary (docs/CONTEXT.md): the action is Mark complete, the thing being finished is a Path of Units and Lessons, and the only streak worth celebrating is the Daily streak — the Path streak is deliberately quiet (Phase 5 PRD §4.3), so it stays off these screens.

Where it stands today

The completed state is one component for every lesson in the product. Finish lesson 3 of 24 and finish lesson 24 of 24 and you get the same card, including “Head back to your path to keep going” — at the exact moment there is nothing left to keep going to. The path-level acknowledgement (CompleteBanner, “Path complete.”) does exist, but it lives on the path view, so the learner only meets it after tapping away from the thing they just finished. The moment and its acknowledgement are one screen apart.

  1. 0–200ms. The button's own press state resolves. Nothing else moves yet — the tap has to feel like it worked before it feels like it mattered.
  2. 200–1300ms. The ring draws. This is the whole ceremony; it is one stroke-dashoffset transition and it degrades to “the ring is just there” under prefers-reduced-motion.
  3. 1300–1500ms. The glyph sets, one glow pulse, done. No loop, no shimmer, nothing that keeps asking for attention after the point is made.
  4. 1500ms+. Copy, stats and doors rise in together. From here the screen is ordinary UI again — scrollable, tappable, and with the drafts block exactly where a learner expects it.

The whole thing is under 1.6 seconds and involves no new dependency: a circle, a transform, two fades, and numbers that count. It fires once per path, ever.

The copy is half the fix

Before any of the motion is built, the final lesson needs to stop saying the mid-path thing. This much is a one-branch change to CompletedState and is worth shipping on its own.

Today, on lesson 24 of 24

“Lesson complete. Nice work. Head back to your path to keep going.”

Points the learner at a path with nothing left in it, and calls the end of twenty-four lessons the same thing it called the end of the first one.

Proposed

“Path complete. You finished Rust ownership — 24 lessons over 11 days.”

Names the path, states the shape of the work, and hands off to somewhere that exists. Specific numbers, no exclamation mark — the product doesn't shout anywhere else.

What the screens need, and what exists

Directions A, B and the copy fix need nothing new. The receipt is where cost appears, and it appears in exactly two rows.

The last row matters most. The client could infer path completion by waiting for the invalidated path detail to come back — but that's a round trip after the tap, so the card would render the mid-path copy first and then change its mind. Returning path_complete (and the two stats with it) from the completion call is what lets the celebration fire on the same frame as the tap, which is the entire point.

Nocturne tokens are lifted unchanged from tailwind.config.ts; the phone chrome and page furniture follow the other mocks in this folder. Every animation here honours prefers-reduced-motion by resolving instantly to its end state — nothing in these screens is only legible in motion.
