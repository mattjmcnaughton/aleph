Your cards

Phase 3 shipped one way to see a card: wait for it to come due. This adds the other one — every kept card, newest first, searchable, editable, deletable. Three screens: the list, fixing a card, and getting rid of one. Drawn in Nocturne beside the flashcards mock, because it is an amendment to that drawing rather than a new one.

This reverses two Phase 3 PRD §7 exclusions — "editing a kept card" and "a card-management or browse-all-cards page" — and the TDD §2 line that records "no card-management surface" as a named absence. Where this drawing and the PRD disagree, the PRD wins. The four pins mark the decisions each screen encodes; all four are settled, and collected at the foot of the page.

Decisions

What this amendment settles

Two of these change the data model rather than the screen, and both belong in the Phase 3 TDD next to the decisions they amend. The rest are product restraint, and belong in the PRD's §7 where the exclusion used to be.

5 Delete is soft

flashcard_reviews is ON DELETE CASCADE from flashcards. A hard delete takes the review log with the card — and the daily streak's active days are a union that reads that log.

Settled: a deleted_at column. Deleting a card you reviewed for a week must not shorten the streak that week earned. It also keeps recall-rate-by-rung honest and preserves the rebuildable-projection guarantee.

4 Edits are stamped

One table holds drafted and kept cards, partly so the flashcard_draft eval can sample what the agent actually produced. Learner-writable text breaks that sampling silently — the eval would start grading the learner's prose as the agent's.

Settled: an edited_at stamp, and eval sampling excludes rows that carry one.

3 Editing does not reschedule

Rung and due date are a record of what the learner knows. Rewording a card changes what it asks, slightly — it does not change what they know, and a reset would make fixing the agent's typo cost a week of progress.

Settled: front, back, edited_at — and nothing else. The scheduler never hears about an edit.

1 Kept cards only

A pending draft is a proposal that belongs to the lesson that produced it, and it already has a surface. Listing proposals beside cards would put two different things in one list and invite a third verb — un-discard.

Settled: the list is kept cards. Manual authoring, un-discarding a draft, and suspend/leech handling all stay out.

Quiet and complete

One confirm, then gone. No archive view, no undo bar, no "cards deleted" counter, no trash. The soft delete is an implementation detail the learner is never shown — promising recovery is a feature, and this is not shipping one.

Settled: the confirm is the whole ceremony. Inherits Phase 5's restraint rule, like the rest of the phase.

6 Two doors, no third

Home's Due today card and the session-complete screen — the two moments the learner is already thinking about cards. PRD §3 is explicit that the due pill is the one piece of persistent navigation the phase adds, and this does not reopen it.

Settled: route /cards, two links in, and no app-bar item.
