What does extends mean in <T extends X>?

It constrains T — T must be assignable to X. It is not class inheritance.

Why constrain a generic at all?

So the body can rely on a property existing — T.length only typechecks once T is constrained.

What is a generic?

A type parameter that varies by call site.

Default type parameter syntax?

<T extends X = Y> — Y is used when the call site names nothing.

Navigation

One schedule, three doors, and a filter

Aleph has no tab bar — the header is a glyph and a sign-out, and the routes are /, /new, /paths/$id, /lessons/$id. So review has to be given a door rather than slotted into one that exists.

Door 1 — Home

The Due today card, above the path list. The primary door: between lessons, this is the reason to open the app at all, so it outranks the paths it sits over.

Route: /review · drawn in screen 02

Door 2 — The header pill

A 10 due pill in the app bar, on every screen. The only persistent navigation the feature adds — and the only way to reach review from inside a lesson without abandoning it via home.

Route: /review · drawn in screens 01 and 02

Door 3 — A path

The path view offers Review this path, and the home list's per-path chip does the same. This is a filter over the one queue, not a second queue — the schedule underneath is identical.

Route: /review?path=… · the chip in screen 02

Can you change scope from inside?

No — scope is set at the door, not in the session. The screen shows which scope you are in (All paths) but offers no switcher: 4 of 12 is a contract with the learner, and changing the denominator underneath them breaks it.

The exception is the end of a filtered session, where widening is what you actually want — so that is the one place it is offered. Mid-session switching follows Phase 2's precedent and waits.

2 So: global, with a path filter

The schedule is global — one due date per card, one queue, one daily habit, matching the Daily streak that just launched. Mixing paths in one session is interleaving, which retains better than drilling one path at a time.

Per-path stays a view, never a schedule. Two schedules would be two streaks, two habits, and two things to keep honest.

Decisions

Settled, and still open

Each of these changes the data model, not just the screen. The four settled ones are written up in the Phase 3 PRD; the open ones are its §8.

2 A card belongs to the learner

Habagou keys review state to a fixed shared curriculum. Aleph has none — paths are generated per learner and 2B lets learners mutate them — so a card cannot be owned by a lesson that may not survive it.

Settled: learner-owned, one global queue. Forced by the global schedule; a per-path queue would split one habit three ways.

3 A ladder, and two grades

A fixed Leitner ladder on a binary signal: Again demotes a rung, Got it promotes one. Habagou's shipped scheduler — a pure function with nothing to tune, which is right when there is no review data to tune against.

Settled. The daily cap argues for it too: with a cap, due is advisory, and a ladder shrugs at a late review where ease factors must react to it.

Ten a day — 7 overdue, 3 at random

Ten cards is the whole day. Over ten due, the queue takes the 7 most overdue plus 3 drawn at random from the rest; ten or fewer, it takes them all. Nothing is pulled forward to fill a short day.

The 3 are anti-starvation: without them the oldest cards monopolise every session and a mid-aged card is never seen again. Home shows the ten, never the backlog.

4 A card outlives its lesson

2B can revise a lesson, Phase 4 will remove and reorder them, and a path can be deleted outright. The card still reviews — its front and back stand alone — and the source line stops being a link.

Settled: survive, degrade the citation. Note this differs from the streak, where deleting a path really does erase its days.

1 A review keeps the streak alive

An Active day is now a day you completed a lesson or reviewed a card. Otherwise clearing the whole daily queue and starting no lesson would break a streak you were actively maintaining — a retention feature punishing retention.

Settled, and free to settle now: no review exists yet, so widening the definition adds no past active day and moves no live streak. Global streak only — never the path streak.

Open — is the debt ever visible?

Hiding the backlog is right for a learner returning after a week away and wrong for one who wants to know where they stand. There is no stats view to put it in yet.

Probably decided with the rest of Phase 5 — the goal ring and daily minutes — rather than here. PRD §8.2.
